Data Deletion (English)

Rafiq — point-of-sale and accounting software by ProAssets — Digital Solutions (Sana'a, Yemen).

To delete your data: open Rafiq → Settings → Account → “Delete shop data”, or email proassets.solutions@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”, or message +967 782 557 180.

We delete all shop records, items, invoices, customer balances and phone numbers, and the message log, and the owner and staff accounts. Nothing is retained after deletion except records we are legally required to keep, which we will list to you in writing at the time of deletion. Requests are completed within 30 days of verifying account ownership, and you receive a confirmation when it is done.

If you are a customer of a shop: ask the shop to stop messages, or contact us and we add your number to a block list immediately.
