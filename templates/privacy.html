Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Summary: We take your privacy seriously. We collect only necessary information to provide our services and never share your data without your consent.

1. Introduction

ProAssets ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ProAssets platform. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

Personal Information

We collect the following personal information:

  • Name and email address

  • Profile information (optional)
  • Usage data and analytics

Automatic Information

We automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining our service
  • Processing transactions and sending related information

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Password hashing with Werkzeug
  • Regular security audits
  • Secure database connections
  • Limited access to sensitive data

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for payments, storage, and analytics. These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
