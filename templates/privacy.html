Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Important platform note

This policy describes the current ProAssets application. Confirm the legal entity, jurisdiction, contact details, and any hosting-provider terms before public launch; this page is not legal advice.

1. Information handled by the platform

Depending on how you use the service, ProAssets stores account details such as username, email address, and optional profile information; product listings and uploaded files; cart and purchase-request records; order status and administrator-entered transaction references; and messages you submit through the contact form.

Passwords are stored as one-way password hashes rather than readable passwords. The hosting provider may process technical request data (for example, IP address, browser, and access logs) under its own terms.

2. Manual payments

This version does not collect card numbers, CVV codes, or PayPal credentials and does not automatically charge a payment method. Paid checkout creates a pending request; payment is arranged outside the app with support, and an administrator may record a payment reference when confirming receipt. Do not send card or banking credentials through the website or contact form.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts and creator listings, respond to messages, manage purchase requests, provide access to completed purchases, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. The current application does not send marketing email or run an in-app advertising tracker.

4. Sharing and storage

Public product pages display the information a creator chooses to publish. Data is stored in the application database and private upload storage configured by the operator. Hosting and storage providers may process data to deliver the service. ProAssets does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

5. Cookies and local storage

The site uses a session cookie to maintain sign-in state. The language preference is saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear these through your browser settings; clearing the session cookie signs you out.

6. Retention and your choices

Account, product, and order records are retained as needed to operate the marketplace and meet legal or accounting obligations. To ask a question or request correction or deletion, contact the platform operator through the contact page. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page. The operator should publish a monitored privacy contact before launch.
